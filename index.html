The visibility gap

Your customers are searching. What do they find?

A strong service is only part of the picture. If your online presence feels incomplete or difficult to use, potential customers may move on before you ever hear from them.

01

An outdated—or missing—website

Your site should quickly explain what you do, where you work and how a customer can take the next step.

02

Few or inconsistent reviews

Without a steady, ethical review process, happy customers may never share the experience that helps others choose you.

03

An incomplete Google profile

Incorrect details, weak categories or missing services can make it harder for customers to understand your business.

04

Too many barriers to enquire

If visitors cannot quickly call, request a quote or book, valuable attention is lost at the moment it matters.

05

Competitors look more trustworthy online

Even when your work is excellent, a clearer and more credible online presence can influence who gets the first call.
